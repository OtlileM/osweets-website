# osweets-website
# ST10502463 Shiko-Otlile Motaung 
# O Sweets is a South African sweets and dessert business that provides a variety of sweet products for customers. The business offers products such as sweets and customised treats that can be ordered for birthdays, parties, celebrations and other special occasions.The purpose of this project is to develop a professional and user-friendly website for O Sweets. The website will provide customers with information about the business, allow them to view available products, see examples of previous work and make enquiries or order requests.The website will also help O Sweets establish a stronger online presence and make its products easier for potential customers to find. The website will be designed to work on computers, tablets and mobile devices.
# The main goal of the O Sweets website is to provide customers with an easy and convenient way to learn about the business, view its products and make enquiries.

The objectives of the website are to:

- Increase the online visibility of O Sweets.
- Display products in a clear and attractive manner.
- Provide customers with information about the business.
- Allow customers to make product and custom-order enquiries.
- Promote products for birthdays, parties and other events.
- Provide contact information in one convenient location.
- Create a professional online image for O Sweets.
# The O Sweets website will contain the following main features:

Home Page

The homepage will introduce O Sweets and display featured products. It will also provide quick links to the products, services and contact sections.

About Us

This page will provide information about O Sweets, including its background, mission and vision.

Products

The products page will display the different sweet products available, including cakes, cupcakes, sweets and other treats. Product descriptions and prices can be included where applicable.

Services

This section will explain the services provided by O Sweets, including customised orders and party or event orders.

Gallery

The gallery will display photographs of cakes, sweets, previous orders and events. This will allow potential customers to see examples of the products.

Contact / Order

Customers will be able to view the business contact details and submit an enquiry or order request.

Order / Enquiry Form

The form will allow customers to provide information such as their name, contact details, product required, preferred date and additional requirements.
